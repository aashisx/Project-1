About Our Project

Welcome to our **Online Ticketing and Vehicle Rental System**, a modern web-based platform designed to simplify booking and renting vehicles online. With just a few clicks, users can book rental cars, motorbikes, or buses, and reserve travel tickets with ease.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between passengers and transportation providers using efficient, user-friendly digital tools. Whether it's a quick city ride or an intercity journey, our system ensures smooth and smart planning.
